### Bringing education, technology, and collaboration together.

Welcome to our GitHub community – a space for learning, sharing knowledge, and building projects together.

## About the College

Halytsky applied college named after Vyacheslav Chornovil is an educational institution in Ternopil, Ukraine. Founded in September 1997, the college combines a respect for tradition with a commitment to innovation, professional development, and modern education.

The college has borne the name of Viacheslav Chornovil since January 2003.

Our educational community spans a range of disciplines, including:

- Computer and publishing technologies
- Law
- Economics and tourism
- Design
- Vocational education

With modern laboratories, computer classrooms, and a dedicated teaching community, the college helps students develop the knowledge and practical skills they need for their professional futures.

## Our GitHub Community

This organization provides a space for students and educators to connect through technology.

Our goals are to:

- Support hands-on learning and project-based education.
- Share code, educational resources, and technical knowledge.
- Encourage collaboration between students and educators.
- Turn ideas into practical tools and digital solutions.
- Foster curiosity, creativity, and continuous improvement.
